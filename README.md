# ZVUK

> an ever-changing electronic soundscape

ZVUK is a generative music experience inspired by kosmische musik, krautrock, Berlin-school sequencing, and vintage analog synthesizers.

Named after the Bosnian word for sound, ZVUK creates warm, atmospheric electronic music that evolves continuously without relying on fixed arrangements or obvious loops.

## Features

- **Generative audio** — evolving harmonies, sequences, and textures.
- **Analog-inspired sound** — warm oscillators, spacious delays, and ambient reverberation.
- **Intuitive controls** — adjust space, motion, pulse, warmth, density, and harmony.
- **Mutate** — introduce musical variations while preserving continuity.
- **Minimal visualization** — subtle, sound-reactive abstract animations.
- **Atmospheric presets** — explore different sonic moods.

## Philosophy

*Controlled randomness + musical memory + slow evolution.*

ZVUK is designed to be listened to rather than programmed. Press play, adjust the atmosphere, and let the music unfold.
